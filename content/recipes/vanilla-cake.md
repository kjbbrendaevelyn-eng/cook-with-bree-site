## Ingredients

### Cake

- 2 1/2 cups (310 g) all-purpose flour
- 2 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 cup (225 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 1 3/4 cups (350 g) granulated sugar
- 4 large eggs, room temperature
- 1 tbsp pure vanilla extract (or 2 tsp vanilla bean paste)
- 1 cup (240 ml) whole milk, room temperature
- 1/2 cup (120 g) sour cream, room temperature

### Vanilla buttercream

- 1 cup (225 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 4 cups (480 g) powdered sugar, sifted
- 2–3 tbsp heavy cream or milk
- 2 tsp pure vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. **Prep:** Preheat oven to **350°F (175°C)**. Grease and flour two **8-inch** or **9-inch** round cake pans (or line the bottoms with parchment).
2. **Dry ingredients:** In a medium bowl, whisk flour, baking powder, and salt.
3. **Cream:** In a large bowl, beat butter and sugar until very light and fluffy, about **3–4 minutes**. This step builds the soft crumb, so don't rush it.
4. **Add eggs and vanilla:** Beat in the eggs one at a time, scraping down the bowl as you go. Mix in the vanilla until the batter smells like a bakery.
5. **Combine:** Mix the milk and sour cream together in a measuring jug. Add the dry ingredients to the butter mixture in three additions, alternating with the milk mixture, beginning and ending with flour. Mix on low just until smooth — stop as soon as you don't see flour streaks.
6. **Bake:** Divide the batter evenly between the pans and smooth the tops. Bake **28–32 minutes**, until a toothpick inserted in the centre comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs. Cool in the pans **10 minutes**, then turn out onto a rack to cool completely.
7. **Buttercream:** Beat butter until creamy, about **2 minutes**. Add powdered sugar one cup at a time, then cream, vanilla, and salt. Beat on medium-high until light and fluffy, **2–3 minutes**. If it's too thick, add a splash more cream; if too soft, chill for **10 minutes**.
8. **Assemble:** Place one cake layer on a plate. Spread a generous layer of buttercream. Top with the second layer and frost the top and sides. Smooth with a spatula, or swirl for a homemade look. Chill briefly before slicing if the kitchen is warm.

## Bree's Notes

This is my go-to vanilla cake — the one I bake for birthdays, quiet Sundays, and anyone who needs a little sweetness. The sour cream is the secret: it keeps the crumb soft for days without making the cake dense. Use real vanilla extract, or vanilla bean paste if you want those little black flecks that make people think you went to pastry school. Room-temperature ingredients matter here; cold eggs or milk will make the batter look curdled and the cake bake unevenly. The layers freeze beautifully wrapped tightly — frost when thawed. For cupcakes, bake about **18–20 minutes**. And if you're feeling fancy, sandwich a thin layer of jam or lemon curd between the layers before the buttercream.
