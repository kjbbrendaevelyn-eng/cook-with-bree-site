## Ingredients

### Cookies

- 1 cup (225 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 3/4 cup (150 g) granulated sugar
- 2 tbsp lemon zest (from about 2 lemons)
- 1 large egg, room temperature
- 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 1/4 cups (280 g) all-purpose flour
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp salt

### Lemon glaze

- 1 1/2 cups (180 g) powdered sugar, sifted
- 3 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 tsp lemon zest, plus extra for topping
- 1 tbsp butter, melted

## Instructions

1. **Rub the zest into the sugar:** In a large bowl, rub the lemon zest into the sugar with your fingertips for about **1 minute**, until the sugar is fragrant and slightly damp. This is where the big lemon flavour comes from.
2. **Cream:** Add the softened butter and beat until light and fluffy, about **2–3 minutes**.
3. **Add the wet ingredients:** Beat in the egg, lemon juice, and vanilla until smooth. Don't worry if it looks a little curdled at first; it comes together once the flour goes in.
4. **Add the dry ingredients:** In another bowl, whisk flour, baking powder, and salt. Add to the butter mixture and mix on low just until a soft dough forms.
5. **Chill:** Cover and refrigerate the dough for at least **30 minutes**, until firm enough to roll.
6. **Prep to bake:** Preheat oven to **350°F (175°C)**. Line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
7. **Shape:** Roll the dough into **1-inch** balls and place them **2 inches** apart on the sheets. Flatten each one slightly with the bottom of a glass.
8. **Bake:** Bake one sheet at a time for **10–12 minutes**, until the bottoms are lightly golden and the tops are set but still pale. Let them cool on the sheet for **5 minutes**, then move them to a wire rack to cool completely.
9. **Glaze:** Whisk powdered sugar, lemon juice, zest, and melted butter until smooth and pourable. Add a few drops more juice if it's too thick, or a spoon more sugar if it's too thin.
10. **Finish:** Dip the top of each cooled cookie into the glaze (or spoon it over), let the excess drip off, and sprinkle with a little extra zest. Let the glaze set for about **20 minutes** before serving or stacking.

## Bree's Notes

These are my "someone's coming over for tea" cookies: small, pretty, and gone in minutes. Rubbing the zest into the sugar is the step I'd never skip. It wakes up all the lemon oils, so the cookies taste like sunshine instead of just sweet. Pull them out while the tops are still pale; once they brown, they lose that soft, melt-in-your-mouth bite. The glaze sets into a thin, crackly shell, so they travel well in a tin. They're lovely with black tea, even better with a squeeze of lemon in your cup.
