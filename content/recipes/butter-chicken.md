## Ingredients

### For the chicken

- 1 1/2 lb (about 700 g) boneless chicken thighs, cut into bite-size pieces
- 1/2 cup plain yogurt
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp Kashmiri chili powder or sweet paprika
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil, for cooking

### For the butter sauce

- 4 tbsp (60 g) butter, divided
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 1/2 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp Kashmiri chili powder or sweet paprika
- 1/2 tsp ground coriander
- 1 can (14 oz / 400 g) tomato purée or crushed tomatoes
- 1/2 cup water or chicken stock
- 1 tsp sugar or honey
- 3/4 cup heavy cream
- 1 tbsp dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi), crushed between your palms (optional but lovely)
- Salt, to taste
- Fresh cilantro, chopped, for serving

## Instructions

1. **Marinate:** In a bowl, mix yogurt, lemon juice, garam masala, cumin, chili powder, turmeric, garlic, ginger, and salt. Add the chicken and coat well. Cover and refrigerate at least **30 minutes** (or overnight for the most tender chicken).
2. **Sear the chicken:** Heat the oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken in batches until browned in spots, about **5–6 minutes**. It doesn't need to be cooked through — it will finish in the sauce. Set aside on a plate.
3. **Build the base:** Lower the heat to medium and melt **2 tbsp** of the butter in the same pan. Add the onion and cook until soft and golden, about **8 minutes**. Stir in garlic and ginger and cook **1 minute**.
4. **Toast the spices:** Add garam masala, cumin, chili powder, and coriander. Stir for **30 seconds** until fragrant — don't let them burn.
5. **Simmer the sauce:** Pour in the tomato purée and water. Add the sugar or honey. Simmer **10–12 minutes**, stirring now and then, until thickened and darker in colour. For an extra-smooth sauce, blend it with a stick blender (or carefully in a blender) and return it to the pan.
6. **Make it buttery:** Stir in the cream, the remaining **2 tbsp** butter, and the kasuri methi. Return the chicken and any juices to the pan. Simmer gently **8–10 minutes**, until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce is silky. Season with salt.
7. **Serve:** Spoon over basmati or coconut rice, or serve with warm chapati or naan. Finish with fresh cilantro and a little swirl of cream if you like.

## Bree's Notes

Butter chicken is the gentle cousin of my chicken tikka masala — milder, creamier, and a little sweet, which makes it the one the kids (and the friends who "don't do spicy") always ask for. The butter goes in twice on purpose: once to soften the onions and once at the end, so the sauce tastes rich and glossy. Kasuri methi is the secret smell of a restaurant butter chicken; if you can't find it, it's still delicious without. Thighs stay juicy even if you get distracted. And please make extra chapati — there's never enough to wipe the pan clean.
