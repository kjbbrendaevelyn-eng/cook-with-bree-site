## Ingredients

- 120 mL warm milk
- 9 g active dry yeast (about 9 g)
- 10 mL honey
- 5 large eggs (you can also use 4 large eggs)
- 1 large egg yolk from a large egg (use 2 egg yolks if using 4 large eggs)
- 10 mL vanilla
- 60 g granulated white sugar (5 tbsp)
- 500 g AP flour (4 cups + 2 tbsp, measured by spoon and level method)
- 1 1/2 tsp sea salt, fine grind
- 250 g unsalted butter, very soft, roughly divided into four portions (1 cup + 2 tbsp)

## Instructions

### Overview

This is a **two-day recipe** that makes **2 loaves** — one Nantaise-style loaf (8 dough balls) and one braided loaf.

| Stage | Time |
|---|---|
| Day 1: mix, knead, first rise | ~1.5 hours active |
| Day 1: chill | 8–24 hours |
| Day 2: shape, proof, bake | ~3 hours |

---

### Day One — Make the Dough

#### 1. Activate the yeast
Whisk **milk, yeast, and honey** in a stand mixer bowl. Rest **10 minutes** until foamy.

#### 2. Mix the dough
Add **eggs and yolk**; whisk to break up the yolks. Add **vanilla, sugar, flour, and salt**. Mix until a shaggy dough forms.

#### 3. Knead and add butter (stand mixer with dough hook)
- Knead on **speed 2–3** for **3–5 minutes**.
- Add **butter in 4 portions**, one portion at a time. Knead **2–3 minutes** after each addition (~**9–12 minutes** total). Scrape the bowl between additions.
- After all butter is in, knead **5–15 minutes** on **speed 5–6** (up to 30 minutes on lower speed).

**Dough is ready when:** it lifts cleanly off the bowl bottom, feels soft and tacky, and does **not** stick to your fingertips.

#### 4. First rise
- Turn dough onto a floured surface. Fold edges to center, flip seam-side down, and shape into a tight ball.
- Place in a large bowl. Cover and rise in a **warm spot (~72°F / 22°C)** until **doubled**, about **1 hour**.

#### 5. Chill overnight
- Deflate gently on a floured surface. Fold edges to center again, flip seam-side down, and reshape into a tight ball.
- Return to bowl, cover, and refrigerate **8–24 hours**.

---

### Day Two — Shape, Proof & Bake

#### 6. Prep
- Remove chilled dough from fridge.
- Butter and flour **two 8.5 × 4 inch loaf pans** (9 × 5 inch pans also work).
- Have ready: bowl of flour, pastry brush, dough scraper, and plastic wrap.

#### 7. Divide the dough
Turn dough out, deflate lightly, and **weigh**. Cut into **2 equal portions** — one per loaf.

---

#### Loaf 1 — Nantaise Brioche (8 dough balls)

1. Weigh one portion and divide into **8 equal pieces**.
2. For each piece: smooth side down → fold edges to center → flip → roll into a tight ball with a cupped palm.
3. Arrange **8 balls in a zig-zag** in one loaf pan (snug fit). Flatten slightly.
4. Cover with plastic wrap and set aside.

---

#### Loaf 2 — Braided Brioche

1. Divide the second portion into **3 equal pieces**.
2. Flatten each into a rectangle → roll tightly into a tube → roll into a **14-inch rope** with even thickness. Repeat for all 3.
3. Pinch one end of the 3 ropes together. **Braid** without stretching or creating loose folds. Loaf should be **9–10 inches** long.
4. Pinch the other end. Flatten and tuck both ends under for a rounded loaf.
5. Transfer to the second pan. Flatten slightly to fit. Cover with plastic wrap.

---

#### 8. Proof
Proof both covered loaves at **room temperature** for about **2 hours**, until **doubled** and nearly at the top of the pan.

**Before baking:** preheat oven to **325°F (163°C)**.

#### 9. Bake
- Brush tops with **egg wash**. Optional: sprinkle **Swedish sugar pearls**.
- Bake until **golden brown** and internal temp reads **190°F (88°C)** — about **35–45 minutes**.

#### 10. Cool & store
Rest in pan **5 minutes**, then turn out onto a rack. Cool completely. Store in an airtight container.

## Bree's Notes

- **Don't rush the butter.** Each portion must fully mix in before adding the next — that's what gives brioche its rich, tender crumb.
- **Judge doneness by feel, not the clock** when kneading. The dough should pass the fingertip test before you move on.
- The **overnight chill** makes Day Two shaping much easier — don't skip it.
