## Ingredients

### Cookies

- 1/2 cup (95 g) shortening
- 1 cup (200 g) granulated sugar
- 1 tbsp orange zest (from about 1 large orange)
- 2 large eggs, room temperature
- 1/2 tbsp fresh orange juice
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 1/2 cups (315 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp salt

### Orange glaze

- 2 cups (240 g) confectioners' sugar
- 1 tsp orange zest
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) orange juice
- 2 tbsp butter, melted
- Orange food coloring, optional

### Optional drizzle

- 1/3 cup (60 g) white chocolate, melted

## Instructions

1. **Prep:** Preheat oven to **350°F (175°C)**. Line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
2. **Rub the zest into the sugar:** In a large bowl, rub the **1 tbsp** orange zest into the **1 cup** sugar with your fingertips for about **1 minute**, until it smells like an orange grove.
3. **Cream:** Add the shortening and beat until light and fluffy, about **2–3 minutes**.
4. **Add the wet ingredients:** Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the **1/2 tbsp** orange juice and the vanilla, until smooth.
5. **Add the dry ingredients:** In another bowl, whisk flour, baking powder, and salt. Add to the shortening mixture and mix on low just until a soft dough forms and no flour streaks remain.
6. **Shape:** Scoop **1 1/2 tbsp** portions of dough, roll into balls, and place them **2 inches** apart on the sheets. Flatten each one slightly with your palm.
7. **Bake:** Bake one sheet at a time for **9–11 minutes**, until the bottoms are lightly golden and the tops are set but still pale.
8. **Cool:** Let the cookies rest on the sheet for **5 minutes**, then move them to a wire rack to cool completely before glazing.
9. **Make the glaze:** Whisk confectioners' sugar, **1 tsp** orange zest, **1/4 cup** orange juice, and melted butter until smooth. Add a drop or two of orange food coloring if you'd like a brighter colour. If it's too thick, add a few drops more juice; if it's too thin, add a spoon more sugar.
10. **Glaze:** Dip the top of each cooled cookie into the glaze (or spread it on with a spoon) and let the excess drip off. Let the glaze set for about **20 minutes**.
11. **Drizzle (optional):** Once the glaze is set, drizzle with melted white chocolate and let it firm up before stacking.

## Bree's Notes

Oranges were always in a bowl on our kitchen table in Buziga, so these cookies taste a bit like home to me. Shortening keeps them soft, puffy, and cake-like, and they stay that way for days. Rubbing the zest into the sugar wakes up the orange oils, so the flavour comes through in every bite, not just the glaze. Let the cookies cool completely before glazing, or the glaze will melt right off. The food coloring is optional, but a little gives them a cheerful sunset glow on a plate next to my lemon tea cookies. They keep in an airtight tin for up to 5 days; layer them with parchment so the glaze doesn't stick.
