## Ingredients

### Cake

- 2 1/2 cups (310 g) all-purpose flour, plus 1 tbsp for tossing the berries
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 cup (225 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 1 3/4 cups (350 g) granulated sugar
- 2 tbsp lemon zest (from about 2 large lemons)
- 4 large eggs, room temperature
- 1/3 cup (80 ml) fresh lemon juice
- 2 tsp pure vanilla extract
- 1 cup (240 g) sour cream, room temperature
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) milk, room temperature
- 2 cups (280 g) fresh blueberries (or frozen, unthawed)

### Lemon cream cheese frosting

- 8 oz (225 g) cream cheese, softened
- 1/2 cup (115 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 3 1/2 cups (420 g) powdered sugar, sifted
- 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 tsp lemon zest
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt

### To finish

- Extra blueberries and lemon zest, for decorating

## Instructions

1. **Prep:** Preheat oven to **350°F (175°C)**. Grease and flour two **8-inch** or **9-inch** round cake pans (or line the bottoms with parchment).
2. **Dry ingredients:** In a medium bowl, whisk flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
3. **Rub the zest into the sugar:** In a large bowl, rub the lemon zest into the sugar with your fingertips for about **1 minute**, until fragrant. Beat in the butter until light and fluffy, about **3 minutes**.
4. **Add eggs and flavour:** Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the lemon juice and vanilla. The batter may look a little curdled — that's fine.
5. **Combine:** Mix the sour cream and milk together. Add the dry ingredients to the butter mixture in three additions, alternating with the sour cream mixture, beginning and ending with flour. Mix on low just until combined.
6. **Fold in the blueberries:** Toss the blueberries with the extra **1 tbsp** flour, then gently fold them into the batter. The flour coating helps keep them from sinking.
7. **Bake:** Divide the batter evenly between the pans. Bake **28–34 minutes**, until a toothpick inserted in the centre comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs. Cool in the pans **10 minutes**, then turn out onto a rack to cool completely.
8. **Frosting:** Beat cream cheese and butter until smooth. Add powdered sugar gradually, then lemon juice, zest, vanilla, and salt. Beat until creamy. If it's too soft, chill **15–20 minutes**.
9. **Assemble:** Place one cake layer on a plate. Spread a generous layer of frosting. Top with the second layer and frost the top and sides. Decorate with extra blueberries and a sprinkle of lemon zest. Chill briefly before slicing if the kitchen is warm.

## Bree's Notes

This is summer on a plate — tart lemon, sweet blueberries, and a frosting that tastes like cheesecake. The sour cream keeps the crumb soft, and tossing the berries in flour is the little trick that stops them all sinking to the bottom. Fresh blueberries are best, but frozen work if you don't thaw them first. Rubbing the zest into the sugar is the same move I use for my lemon cake and lemon tea cookies: it wakes up the oils so the lemon flavour runs through the whole cake, not just the frosting. The layers freeze well wrapped tightly — frost when thawed. Perfect for birthdays, brunch, or any day that needs a little sunshine.
