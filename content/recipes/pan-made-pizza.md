## Ingredients

### Dough
- 2 cups (250 g) all-purpose flour, plus more for dusting
- 1 tsp instant yeast
- 1 tsp salt
- 3/4 cup (180 ml) warm water
- 1 tbsp olive oil, plus more for the pan

### Sauce & toppings
- 1/2 cup pizza sauce or crushed tomatoes
- 1–1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella
- Toppings of choice: pepperoni, cooked sausage, mushrooms, peppers, onions, olives
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano or Italian seasoning
- Salt and black pepper, to taste
- Fresh basil or chili flakes, for serving (optional)

## Instructions

1. **Make the dough:** In a bowl, mix flour, yeast, and salt. Stir in warm water and olive oil until a shaggy dough forms. Knead on a lightly floured surface for **5–7 minutes** until smooth and elastic. (Or mix in a stand mixer with a dough hook.)
2. **Rise:** Place dough in a lightly oiled bowl, cover, and let rise in a warm spot until doubled, about **45–60 minutes**. For a quicker night, rest at least **20–30 minutes** — it will still bake up well.
3. **Heat the pan:** Generously oil a 10–12 inch cast-iron or heavy skillet. Press the dough into the pan, stretching to the edges. Cover and rest **10 minutes** so it relaxes.
4. **Par-cook the crust:** Set the pan over medium heat. Cook the dough alone for **3–4 minutes**, until the bottom starts to set and lightly brown. Peek underneath — you want pale gold, not burnt.
5. **Top:** Spread sauce over the dough, leaving a small border. Add cheese and toppings. Sprinkle oregano, salt, and pepper.
6. **Finish:** Cover the pan with a lid (or foil). Cook over medium-low heat for **8–12 minutes**, until the cheese is melted and the bottom is crisp and deep golden. If the top needs more melt, cover tightly for the last few minutes.
7. **Serve:** Slide onto a cutting board, rest **1–2 minutes**, slice, and finish with basil or chili flakes if you like.

## Bree's Notes

Pan pizza is my no-oven workaround — crispy bottom, soft middle, and dinner that feels like a treat. Cast iron gives the best crust; a heavy nonstick skillet works too. Don't overload toppings or the center stays soggy. Leftover slices reheat surprisingly well in the same pan for a minute or two.
