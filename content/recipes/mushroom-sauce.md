## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp butter or vegetable oil
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 lb (about 450 g) mushrooms, sliced (button, cremini, or mixed)
- 1 tbsp tomato paste (optional, for deeper color)
- 1 tsp fresh thyme or 1/2 tsp dried thyme
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- 1 cup vegetable or chicken stock
- 1/2 cup heavy cream or full-fat coconut milk
- Salt and black pepper, to taste
- Fresh parsley, chopped, for serving

## Instructions

1. **Sauté the base:** Heat butter or oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add onion and cook until soft and golden, about **6–8 minutes**.

2. Stir in garlic and cook for **1 minute** until fragrant.

3. **Cook the mushrooms:** Add sliced mushrooms with a pinch of salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until they release their liquid and turn golden brown, about **8–10 minutes**. Don't rush — browned mushrooms make the sauce.

4. Stir in tomato paste (if using), thyme, and paprika. Cook for **1 minute**.

5. **Simmer:** Pour in stock and bring to a gentle simmer. Cook for **5 minutes** until slightly reduced.

6. Lower the heat and stir in cream or coconut milk. Simmer another **3–5 minutes** until the sauce is silky and coats the back of a spoon. Season with salt and pepper.

7. **Serve:** Spoon over rice, chapati, pasta, mashed potatoes, or grilled chicken. Finish with fresh parsley.

## Bree's Notes

Mushroom sauce is one of those quiet weeknight heroes — it tastes fancy but comes together in one pan. Coconut milk keeps it dairy-free and pairs beautifully with African-inspired dinners; cream makes it richer for pasta night. If your mushrooms look crowded in the pan, cook them in two batches so they brown instead of steaming.
