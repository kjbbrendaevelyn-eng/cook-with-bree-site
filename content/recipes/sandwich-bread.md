## Ingredients

- 1 cup (240 mL) warm milk (about 110°F / 43°C)
- 2 1/4 tsp (7 g) active dry yeast
- 2 tbsp granulated sugar
- 3 1/4 cups (390 g) bread flour (all-purpose works too)
- 1 1/2 tsp fine sea salt
- 4 tbsp (56 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 1 large egg, room temperature

## Instructions

1. **Activate the yeast:** In a large bowl or stand mixer bowl, whisk together warm milk, yeast, and sugar. Let sit **10 minutes** until foamy on top.

2. **Mix the dough:** Add the egg, flour, and salt. Mix with a wooden spoon or dough hook until a shaggy dough forms.

3. **Knead and add butter:** Knead by hand or on medium speed for **3–4 minutes**. Add softened butter **one tablespoon at a time**, kneading until each addition is absorbed. Continue kneading **6–8 minutes** until the dough is smooth, soft, and slightly tacky but not sticky.

4. **First rise:** Shape into a ball, place in a lightly greased bowl, and cover. Rise in a warm spot until **doubled**, about **1 to 1 1/2 hours**.

5. **Shape the loaf:** Turn dough out onto a lightly floured surface. Press to deflate, then shape into a smooth log about **9 inches** long. Place seam-side down in a greased **9 × 5 inch** loaf pan.

6. **Second rise:** Cover loosely and let rise until the dough crowns **about 1 inch above** the rim of the pan — about **45 to 60 minutes**.

7. **Bake:** Preheat oven to **350°F (175°C)**. Bake until the top is **golden brown** and the internal temperature reads **190°F (88°C)** — about **30 to 35 minutes**.

8. **Cool:** Rest in the pan **5 minutes**, then turn out onto a wire rack. Cool completely before slicing — warm bread tears, and patience here is worth it.

## Bree's Notes

- **Bread flour** gives a slightly chewier, more structured slice; **all-purpose** makes a softer, more tender loaf. Both are delicious — use what you have.
- If your kitchen is cool, the first rise can take longer. I sometimes put the covered bowl near a warm oven or on top of a heating pad set to low.
- This loaf keeps well for **2–3 days** wrapped at room temperature. For longer storage, slice and freeze — thaw slices at room temperature or toast straight from frozen.
- A thin coat of **melted butter** brushed on top right when it comes out of the oven gives a classic bakery finish.
