## Ingredients

### Cookies

- 6 tbsp (85 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 1/2 cup (100 g) granulated sugar
- 1 large egg yolk, room temperature
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 tsp oil (vegetable or other neutral oil)
- 1 1/2 cups (190 g) all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup (30 g) cornstarch
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp salt

### For filling

- 1/2 cup (160 g) thick jam: strawberry, raspberry, apricot, mango, or passion fruit

### Optional glaze

- 1/2 cup (60 g) powdered sugar
- 2–3 tsp milk

## Instructions

1. **Cream:** In a large bowl, beat butter and sugar until light and fluffy, about **2–3 minutes**.
2. **Add the wet ingredients:** Beat in the egg yolk, vanilla, and oil until smooth.
3. **Add the dry ingredients:** In another bowl, whisk flour, cornstarch, baking powder, and salt. Add to the butter mixture and mix on low until the dough comes together. It will look crumbly at first; squeeze it with your hands and it should hold together like play dough.
4. **Chill:** Cover and refrigerate the dough for **30 minutes**, so the cookies hold their shape in the oven.
5. **Prep to bake:** Preheat oven to **350°F (175°C)**. Line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
6. **Roll:** Roll the dough into **1-inch** balls and place them **2 inches** apart on the sheets.
7. **Make the thumbprints:** Press your thumb (or the back of a small measuring spoon) into the centre of each ball to make a little well. If the edges crack, just pinch them back together.
8. **Fill:** Stir the jam until smooth, then spoon about **1/2 tsp** into each well. Don't overfill; the baking powder makes the cookies puff a little and the jam bubbles up as it bakes.
9. **Bake:** Bake one sheet at a time for **11–13 minutes**, until the bottoms are lightly golden and the cookies are set. Let them cool on the sheet for **5 minutes**, then move them to a wire rack to cool completely.
10. **Glaze (optional):** Whisk powdered sugar and milk into a thin glaze and drizzle over the cooled cookies. Let it set before stacking.

## Bree's Notes

Thumbprint cookies are the best ones to make with kids, because everyone gets to press their own thumb into the dough, and nobody cares if they come out a little wonky. The cornstarch and a splash of oil keep them tender and melt-in-your-mouth, and the egg yolk adds richness without making them cakey. My favourite twist is filling half with mango or passion fruit jam: they taste like a Kampala market in a tiny cookie. Use a thick jam, or warm a runny one in a pan for a few minutes to thicken it, so it stays put in the oven. They keep for up to a week in an airtight tin, and the jam gets even softer by day two.
