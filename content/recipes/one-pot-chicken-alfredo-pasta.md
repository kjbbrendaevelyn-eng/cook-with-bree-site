## Ingredients

- 1 lb (about 450 g) boneless chicken breast or thighs, cut into bite-size pieces
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 cups chicken stock (plus a splash more if needed)
- 2 cups milk (whole preferred)
- 8 oz (about 250 g) fettuccine
- 1 cup freshly grated Parmesan, plus more for serving
- 1/2 tsp Italian seasoning or dried oregano
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- Salt, to taste
- Fresh parsley, chopped, for serving
- Red pepper flakes (optional)

## Instructions

1. **Season the chicken:** Pat dry and season with salt and black pepper.
2. **Brown the chicken:** In a large deep skillet or Dutch oven, heat butter and oil over medium-high heat. Add chicken and cook until golden and cooked through, about **6–8 minutes**. Transfer to a plate.
3. **Build the pot:** Lower heat to medium. Add garlic and cook **30–60 seconds** until fragrant (don't let it burn).
4. Pour in chicken stock and milk. Stir in Italian seasoning, pepper, and a pinch of salt. Bring to a gentle simmer.
5. **Cook the pasta:** Add the pasta, pressing it under the liquid. Simmer uncovered, stirring often so it doesn't stick, until the pasta is al dente and most of the liquid has thickened into a sauce, about **12–15 minutes**. Add a splash more stock or milk if it gets too thick before the pasta is done.
6. **Finish:** Stir in Parmesan until the sauce is creamy. Return the chicken to the pot and toss to coat. Taste and adjust salt and pepper.
7. **Serve:** Spoon into bowls. Top with more Parmesan, parsley, and red pepper flakes if you like.



## Bree's Notes

One-pot alfredo is my weeknight cheat code — same cozy bowl, one pan to wash. Stir often once the pasta goes in; that's what keeps it creamy instead of clumpy. Freshly grated Parmesan melts smoother than the pre-shredded kind. If the sauce looks loose when you turn off the heat, give it a minute — it thickens as it sits. Leftovers reheat with a splash of milk.
