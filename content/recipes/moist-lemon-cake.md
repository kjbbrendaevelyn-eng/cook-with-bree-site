## Ingredients

### Cake

- 1 1/2 cups (190 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 cup (200 g) granulated sugar
- 2 tbsp lemon zest (from about 2 large lemons)
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) vegetable oil
- 2 large eggs, room temperature
- 1/2 cup (120 g) sour cream or full-fat Greek yogurt, room temperature
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) fresh lemon juice
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) milk, room temperature

### Lemon syrup

- 1/4 cup (50 g) granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) fresh lemon juice

### Lemon glaze

- 1 1/4 cups (150 g) powdered sugar, sifted
- 2–3 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 tsp lemon zest

## Instructions

1. **Prep:** Preheat oven to **350°F (175°C)**. Grease a **9×5-inch** loaf pan and line it with parchment, leaving an overhang on the long sides so you can lift the cake out easily.
2. **Dry ingredients:** In a medium bowl, whisk flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
3. **Rub the zest into the sugar:** In a large bowl, rub the lemon zest into the sugar with your fingertips for about **1 minute**, until the sugar is fragrant and slightly damp. This is where the big lemon flavour lives.
4. **Wet ingredients:** Whisk the oil into the lemon sugar until it looks like wet sand. Add the eggs one at a time, then the sour cream, lemon juice, and vanilla. Mix until smooth.
5. **Combine:** Add half the dry ingredients and stir gently. Pour in the milk, then the rest of the dry ingredients. Mix just until no flour streaks remain — a few small lumps are fine. Don't overmix or the cake will turn tough.
6. **Bake:** Scrape the batter into the pan and smooth the top. Bake **45–55 minutes**, until a toothpick inserted in the centre comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs. If the top browns too fast, loosely tent with foil for the last **10 minutes**.
7. **Make the syrup:** While the cake bakes, warm the sugar and lemon juice in a small pan over low heat, stirring until the sugar dissolves. Keep it warm.
8. **Soak:** As soon as the cake comes out of the oven, poke holes all over the top with a skewer or toothpick. Brush or slowly pour the warm syrup over the hot cake, letting it soak in. Cool in the pan for **20 minutes**, then lift out onto a rack to cool completely.
9. **Glaze:** Whisk powdered sugar, lemon juice, and zest until thick but pourable. Spoon it over the cooled cake and let it drip down the sides. Let the glaze set for about **20 minutes** before slicing.

## Bree's Notes

This is the lemon cake I make when I want people to close their eyes after the first bite. The sour cream keeps it soft for days, the oil means it never dries out, and the warm syrup soak is the non-negotiable step — skip it and you'll still have a good cake, but you won't have *the* cake. Rubbing the zest into the sugar is the same trick I use for my lemon tea cookies: it wakes up the oils so the whole loaf tastes like sunshine, not just the glaze. Use real lemons, not bottled juice. The cake keeps well wrapped at room temperature for 3 days, or freeze unglazed slices for up to a month. Serve with tea, or with fresh berries if you want to show off.
