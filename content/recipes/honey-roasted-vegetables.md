## Ingredients

- 2 sweet potatoes, cubed
- 3 carrots, cut into chunks
- 2 parsnips, cut into chunks
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp cumin
- Salt and pepper
- Fresh rosemary

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).

2. Toss all vegetables with olive oil, honey, paprika, cumin, salt, and pepper on a large baking sheet. Spread in a single layer.

3. Roast for 35-40 minutes, flipping halfway, until vegetables are caramelized and tender.

4. Garnish with fresh rosemary and serve warm.

## Bree's Notes

These show up at every holiday dinner I host. The honey and smoked paprika combo is the move — don't skip it.
