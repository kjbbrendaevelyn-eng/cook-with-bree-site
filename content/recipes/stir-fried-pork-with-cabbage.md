## Ingredients

- 1 lb (about 450 g) pork tenderloin or boneless pork chops, thinly sliced
- 1/2 medium head green cabbage, shredded (about 4–5 cups)
- 1 medium onion, thinly sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated (optional)
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce (optional, for deeper savoriness)
- 1 tsp sesame oil (optional)
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/4 tsp chili flakes (optional)
- Salt, to taste
- 2 green onions, sliced, for serving
- Cooked rice, for serving

## Instructions

1. **Prep the pork:** Pat dry and season lightly with salt and black pepper. Toss with 1 tbsp soy sauce and set aside while you prep the vegetables.
2. **Hot pan:** Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat until shimmering.
3. **Cook the pork:** Add pork in a single layer (work in batches if needed). Stir-fry until browned and just cooked through, about **4–5 minutes**. Transfer to a plate.
4. **Cook the cabbage:** Add remaining oil to the pan. Add onion and cook **2 minutes** until softened. Stir in garlic and ginger (if using) for **30 seconds**.
5. Add cabbage with a pinch of salt. Stir-fry until wilted but still a little crisp, about **4–6 minutes**.
6. **Combine:** Return the pork to the pan. Add remaining soy sauce, oyster sauce (if using), sesame oil, and chili flakes. Toss everything together for **1–2 minutes** until glossy and hot. Taste and adjust salt or soy.
7. **Serve:** Spoon over rice and finish with green onions.

## Bree's Notes

This is my "there's cabbage in the fridge and dinner needs to happen" stir-fry. Slice the pork thin so it cooks fast and stays tender — partially freezing it for 15 minutes makes slicing easier. Don't overcrowd the pan or the pork will steam instead of brown. Leftovers reheat well for lunch the next day.
